# BursaryBuddy Navigator

Build a polished, modern, mobile-first application called **BursaryBuddy**.

## 1. PRODUCT VISION

BursaryBuddy is a South African student opportunity platform designed to help students discover, understand, organise, and apply for bursaries without feeling overwhelmed.

The core problem is simple:

Many students struggle to find bursaries that they actually qualify for because opportunities are scattered across different websites, social media posts, PDFs, university pages, and company websites. Students can also miss deadlines, misunderstand eligibility requirements, or forget which documents they need.

BursaryBuddy brings this information into one simple, student-friendly platform.

### Core value proposition

**"Find funding. Track deadlines. Take control of your future."**

The app should feel like a supportive digital assistant rather than a complicated financial platform.

The target users are primarily South African high-school learners, university students, TVET students, and young people looking for education funding.

* Primary colour: deep blue

* Secondary accent: warm gold/yellow

* Background: very light neutral/white

* Text: dark navy/charcoal

Use cards, chips, progress indicators, badges, bottom navigation, and clear call-to-action buttons.

**Your match: 92%**

Then explain why:

✓ Your field matches

✓ Your study level matches

Because bursary scams are a real concern, include a section called:

**Stay Safe**

* Verify opportunities on the official provider's website.

* Never pay someone simply to access a bursary.

* Be careful with requests for sensitive personal information.

* Check application deadlines and requirements directly with the provider.

Add a visible label to opportunities:

**Verified source**

or

**Demo opportunity**

Do not claim that a provider is verified unless verification has actually been performed.

# 28. IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

BursaryBuddy should NOT promise students funding.

It helps students:

**DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → PREPARE → APPLY → TRACK**

The app is an opportunity-management platform, not a bursary provider.

# 30. FINAL QUALITY STANDARD

Before considering the build complete, test the entire user journey.

A new user should be able to:

1. Open BursaryBuddy

2. Create a profile

3. See personalised opportunities

4. Search/filter opportunities

5. Open a bursary

6. Understand the requirements

7. See their match

8. Save the opportunity

9. Start an application

10. Complete a document checklist

11. Track the application

12. See upcoming deadlines

The final product should look like a serious startup MVP that could be demonstrated to investors, educators, students, or potential partners.

Do not make it look like a generic AI-generated dashboard.

Prioritise **clarity, usefulness, trust, accessibility and excellent mobile UX**.
